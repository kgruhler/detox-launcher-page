The Detox Launcher app

The app shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking libraries and has no user account. Settings, favorites and hidden apps are stored only on your device.

Some features are optional and need your permission. You can revoke it at any time in Android's settings. The data is processed only on your device:

  • Usage access for screen time and the weekly chart.
  • Contacts for contact search and for “Your people”.
  • Calendar to show your next event.
  • Notification access for the dots next to “Your people”. The app only checks messenger notifications to see whether one of the people you chose has written. Content is neither stored permanently nor transmitted.

Weather

If you turn on the weather line, the app sends the place name you entered to the Open-Meteo service (open-meteo.com) to find the place, and then that place's coordinates to fetch the weather. Your IP address is transmitted for technical reasons. Your precise location (GPS) is not used. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (a feature you requested). Open-Meteo's terms apply.

Web search

When you search the web from the app, the search engine you chose (Google, DuckDuckGo, Startpage or Ecosia) opens in your browser. That search engine's privacy policy applies.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. For downloads, updates and the one-time rating dialog in the app (Google Play In-App Review), Google processes data under its own privacy policy.
